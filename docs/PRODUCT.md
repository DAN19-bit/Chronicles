# Chronicles — Product Brief

**Status:** Initial working brief; founder review required before major product commitments.

## Product direction
Chronicles is a hybrid Christian testimony archive and community. It helps people share personal testimonies, discover encouraging accounts, and engage respectfully with contributors.

## Core experience
- Publish and preserve testimony stories, with optional photos or videos.
- Browse and search testimonies by topic, category, and date.
- Community profiles, reactions, comments, and witness accounts.
- Clear contributor consent and privacy controls.

## Editorial and trust principles
- A submitted testimony is a personal account; publication does not automatically mean independent verification.
- Distinguish contributor accounts, witness statements, and independently verified information.
- Do not promise healing, guaranteed outcomes, or discourage professional medical care.
- Provide reporting, moderation, correction, and removal pathways.
- Minimize collection and public display of sensitive personal information.

## Initial product questions requiring founder decisions
- Whether anonymous submissions are allowed and under what safeguards.
- Which media types and upload limits are supported at launch.
- Whether witness accounts are public comments, structured attestations, or both.
- Moderation and appeal policy.
- Launch audience, languages, and age policy.

## MVP principle
Build a safe, usable testimony submission and discovery experience before expanding social features. Keep scope small and validate with real users.
